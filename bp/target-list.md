## How to work this list

1. **This week:** email/DM the ⭐ Top 15 in order. Personalize the first line each time; carry the "which gross / which page" insight from `outreach-playbook.md` as your credential.
2. **Segment your questions:** CPA-firm partners → "how do your dealer clients handle month-end close / DMS reconciliation / where the factory statement misleads them." Group CFOs/controllers → their own last-month workflow. Consultants → the patterns they see across many stores.
3. **Every interview ends with "who else should I talk to?"** — the aggregator-flagged (⚠️) contacts and non-public controllers get much easier once you have a referral.
4. **Verify ⚠️ rows before sending** — a 30-second bio-page check avoids a wrong-name open.
5. **Log every contact in the tracker** (`outreach-playbook.md` §6) and review Friday.
